How the SEO Opportunity Score works

The SEO Opportunity Score (0–100) is a proprietary heuristic made for this tool. It is not a Google metric and does not predict rankings or traffic.

  • Impressions (25): log-scaled demand versus your largest row.
  • Potential traffic gain (30): extra clicks if the row reached a modestly better position or its expected CTR. Uses a generic CTR-by-position curve, so treat it as an estimate.
  • Position (18): positions 4–10 score highest, then 11–20, then lower.
  • CTR gap (10): how far actual CTR is below the generic curve.
  • Clicks (7): existing traffic worth protecting or growing.
  • Page/query relationship (10): cannibalization and pages ranking for many related queries score higher.

Priority: High ≥ 70, Medium 45–69, Low < 45. Always validate against the live SERP, search intent and your own data.
